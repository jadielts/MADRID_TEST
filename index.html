    How to read · method

    Scale. Theoretical normalisation, as in PTAL. Each indicator is scored from 0 to 1 between two fixed thresholds: 0 at or below the minimum acceptable value, 1 at or above the reference standard, linear in between. A score therefore does not depend on the other stations or on Lisbon. Thresholds are of three types: T, theoretical scale (yes/no, 0–1 ratio, maximum possible; 21 indicators); L, literature or standard (ITDP, PTAL, PT DL 163/2006; 5); and P, own proposal still to be justified (25). This is version v0 and the P thresholds are under review. Varying the P thresholds by ±25% leaves the ranking almost unchanged (Spearman ≥ 0.999). The info button of each indicator shows its thresholds and caveats. The grey reference is the mean of the 4 pilots.

    Aggregation. As in the AML: each point of view is the mean of its indicators; each dimension is the mean of the indicators of its two points of view; the global score is the mean of the 4 dimensions.

    Interface. In Madrid each pilot is measured as a whole interface (Cercanías, Metro and buses together), not per operator as in Lisbon.

    Measurement areas.

    • directions_walk 5 min walk (4 km/h, ≈330 m): access and transfer experience.
    • directions_walk 10 min walk (≈670 m): what can be reached on foot.
    • directions_bike 10 min by bike (15 km/h): the territory served by the station.
    • location_on Station: field survey and service (GTFS).

    Network position (PoV 2). Stops reached by the same trips that call at the station, without transfer (GTFS Renfe + CRTM). Proposed threshold: 1 from 100 stops. The indicator depends on network size and will be redefined.

    Employment (PoV 4). Madrid has no employment data below municipality level, so these values are estimates: 2025 Social Security affiliates by municipality and sector, distributed over Catastro non-residential floor area (by use) and over open premises in residential buildings (k = 2.06 workers per premise, INE; range 1.78–2.34).

    Known limitations. Metro L3 has no timetables in the CRTM GTFS (Moncloa frequency counts L6 only) · sidewalk width uses OSM default values, as in the AML (not measured) · employment is estimated.

    Proposed sample. Stations with Cercanías and Metro within walking transfer distance (up to ~800 m), as suburban as possible, in two strata by distance to Puerta del Sol: metropolitan (11–17 km: Fuenlabrada, Móstoles, Alcorcón, Coslada, Getafe Centro, Leganés, El Casar and, to the north, Alcobendas-S.S. de los Reyes) and city periphery (7–9 km: Vicálvaro, Mirasierra-Paco de Lucía, Pitis, Cuatro Vientos, Villaverde Alto, Vallecas and Ramón y Cajal). Alcobendas and Ramón y Cajal cover the north, where Metro (L10) is 650–800 m from the Cercanías station; that long transfer is measured rather than used to exclude the station. Excluded: Parla, Aravaca and Fuente de la Mora (Metro Ligero, not Metro), Airport T4 (atypical) and the large central stations. The 4 pilots cover the centre (Moncloa, Príncipe Pío), the periphery (Aluche) and the Cercanías-only ring (Majadahonda). Total: 19 interfaces. Hollow markers show the proposed stations; their 51 indicators have not been surveyed yet.
